# How to Cite AI GOVERNANCE CHAIN™
    
    AI GOVERNANCE CHAIN™ is intended as a citable reference for boards, chief AI officers, compliance leaders, and the architects responsible for building AI that can be trusted. If our frameworks, instruments, or the State of AI Governance report inform your work, please cite them.

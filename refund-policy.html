The short version

  • Instruments, kits, packs and the Atlas: full refund if you ask within 14 days of delivery.
  • Subscriptions and memberships: full refund of the first payment within 14 days, then cancel at any time.
  • Practitioner Programme: full refund until the programme starts, or within 14 days of payment if that is later.
  • To ask, email contact@aigovernancechain.com. We pay you back within 14 days.

1. Instruments, kits, packs and the Atlas

  • Ask within 14 days of the day you receive the files. You do not need to give a reason.
  • We refund everything you paid, including any tax. We do not deduct payment or bank fees.
  • We may ask for one line of feedback. Answering is optional and never a condition of the refund.
  • Once you are refunded, your licence ends. Please delete the files and any copies, and do not use or share them.
  • One refund per product per organisation. If you buy the same product again after a refund, that purchase is final, unless the law gives you more.
  • If a file is damaged, missing or does not match its description, tell us at any time. We fix or replace it, and if we cannot, we refund it in full, even after 14 days.

2. Subscriptions and memberships

This covers the Governance Vault, The Governance Circle, The Living Standard and the Intelligence Subscription.

  • First payment: full refund if you ask within 14 days of paying.
  • Cancel at any time by email. Renewal stops, and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. There is no cancellation fee.
  • After the first 14 days, we do not refund part of a period already paid for.
  • Annual plans: we email you at least 30 days before each renewal, with the price. If a renewal you did not want is charged, tell us within 14 days of the renewal date and we refund it in full.
  • When a subscription is refunded, access ends, and so does the licence for any files downloaded under it.

3. Practitioner Programme

  • Full refund if you ask before the programme starts, or within 14 days of paying, whichever is later.
  • After the programme starts, you can transfer your seat to a colleague, or move once to a later cohort, free of charge.
  • If we cancel or postpone a cohort, you choose: a full refund or a seat in a later cohort.
  • If you are refunded, no completion record is issued and any directory listing is removed.
  • Renewals: full refund if you ask within 14 days of paying.

4. Engagements and written agreements

Engagements above 5,000 USD, and the Large and Enterprise bands of the Governance Vault, begin with a conversation and a written agreement. That agreement sets the refund and cancellation terms. Where it says nothing about refunds, this policy applies.

5. How to ask for a refund

  • Email contact@aigovernancechain.com with the subject "Refund request". Include your order reference, or the email address you used to buy. One sentence is enough.
  • We confirm receipt within 2 working days.
  • We pay the refund within 14 days of your request, to the payment method you used, unless you ask for another method that costs you nothing. The refund is made in the currency you paid in.
  • Your bank or card provider may take a few more days to show the money in your account.

6. Your rights under the law

  • These terms apply to every buyer, organisations and public bodies included. Consumer law usually protects only individuals; we give organisations the same terms.
  • This policy adds to your legal rights and never reduces them. If the law where you live gives you more, that law applies. For example, consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom have 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract, and consumers in Brazil may withdraw within 7 days with an immediate refund under Article 49 of the Consumer Defence Code.
  • Swiss law gives no general right to withdraw from an online purchase. This policy gives you one anyway.
  • Our 14 days apply even after you have downloaded and used what you bought. You do not lose them by opening the files.
  • If something we supply is faulty, the legal remedies where you live apply in addition to this policy.

7. Questions and changes

  • Questions about this policy: contact@aigovernancechain.com.

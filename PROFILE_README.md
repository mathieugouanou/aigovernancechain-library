    ## What I do
    
    I help organizations approaching scale govern AI systems **before** the systems govern them. My focus is the failure mode almost no one names: **sycophantic AI** ,  models that flatter, defer, and confirm rather than tell the truth. In agentic and orchestrated systems, that single bias compounds into cascading, unaudited risk.
    
    I build **human-first governance** that is inspectable, forkable, and board-ready.

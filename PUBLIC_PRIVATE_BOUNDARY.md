# Public and Private Boundary
    
    The public repo gives away the WHAT and the WHY at zero friction. The private and paid layers
    deliver the HOW, the WITH-YOU, and the DONE-FOR-YOU.
    
    Free: thesis, frameworks at overview depth, lead-magnet assessments, the journey map, the glossary.
    Paid: full toolkits, scoring engines, the audit methodology, deliverable templates, advisory.
    
    This is an open-core model. The free layer builds trust and qualifies buyers; the paid layer
    captures the value.

# The Segmentation Matrix (MECE)
    
    A 5 x 2 grid. Five capacity tiers (Micro, Small, Mid, Large, Enterprise/Public) crossed with
    two sectors (Private, Public). Every organization lands in exactly one cell.
    
    ## The overlay model (the guardrail)
    One core asset per discipline, plus a short right-sized profile per segment. Scale the
    positioning, not the page count. Never build 30 assets times 10 segments.
    
    ## Capacity-matched pricing
    The same discipline sells at Micro prices and Enterprise prices, fairly, because it is delivered
    at the right depth. This is the price-discrimination engine, and it unlocks the public sector.

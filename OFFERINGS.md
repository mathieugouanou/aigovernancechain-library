# AI GOVERNANCE CHAIN™: The Value Ladder

Organized on two axes: discipline (what you need) crossed with capacity (who you are).

## Right-size yourself first
| Tier | Headcount | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | 3 to 10 | No governance role; needs a one-person starter |
| Small | 11 to 50 | One person, many hats; lightweight templates |
| Mid | 51 to 250 | First risk hire; a repeatable operating system |
| Large | 251 to 5,000 | Formal functions; cross-unit coordination |
| Enterprise / Public | 5,000+ / ministries | Board and regulators; federated governance |

Crossed with sector: Private and Public.

## Tier A: Free lead magnets (the credibility engine)
Readiness Scorecard, EU AI Act Quick-Classification, ISO 42001 Readiness Checklist,
Agentic AI Risk Quick-Reference, Board-Ready One-Pager, Journey Start-Here map.
Serves all capacity tiers. Earns trust, feeds the funnel.

## Tier B: Low-priced products (29 to 199)
Sycophancy Audit Toolkit, EU AI Act Readiness Pack, ISO/IEC 42001 Implementation Toolkit,
NIST AI RMF Workbook, Policy Template Library, Agentic Guardrails Checklist.
Strongest fit: Micro to Mid.

## Tier C: Premium products (199 to 1,500)
AI Governance Operating System, Agentic AI Governance Framework, AI Architecture Blueprint,
Maturity Model, State of AI Governance Annual Report.
Strongest fit: Mid to Large.

## Tier D: High-value and recurring (advisory-linked)
Pre-Scale Audit, Agentic Design Engagement, the Governance Circle membership,
Practitioner Programme, Retainer, Licensing, Keynotes.
Strongest fit: Large and Enterprise/Public.

## Where the money concentrates
Micro and Small grow volume and pipeline. Public-sector Large and Enterprise grow profit through
audits and retainers. Capacity-matched pricing lets the same IP sell at Micro and Enterprise prices.

# Glossary
    
    Sycophancy (AI): a model producing agreeable, flattering, or consensus-seeking output at the
    expense of truth or sound judgment.
    
    Right-sizing: adapting a governance asset to an organization's capacity tier and sector.
    
    Overlay: a short profile that applies a core asset to one segment without duplicating it.
    
    Journey layer: the lifecycle navigation that sequences assets from Orient to Certify.
    
    Decision trace: an auditable record of how an AI-influenced decision was made and who owned it.
